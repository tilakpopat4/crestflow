## ⚡ Core Features Cheat Sheet (For Video, Carousel & Copy Ads)

### 1. Smart Invoice Generator with Instant Dynamic UPI QR Codes
* **0% Transaction Fee UPI QR**: Automatically embeds client payment amount and UPI ID (`upi://pay?pa=...&am=...`) into an instant, high-contrast QR code on every PDF invoice. Clients scan with Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm and pay directly into your bank.
* **Auto-Billing Format Detection**: Automatically switches between **Monthly Retainer** and **Per-Reel / Per-Deliverable** pricing when you select a client—no manual configuration required.
* **Dynamic Cycle Dates**: Default billing cycle starts from the client's **Previous Payment Date** up to the **Present Date**, eliminating billing overlaps.
* **Smart Auto-Sizing Work Log**: Invoices dynamically scale layout and row heights to fit all project deliverables with zero awkward white space.
* **Clean Invoice Numbering**: Standardized `<CF>-00001` format for clean accounting.
* **Scheduled 6:00 AM Auto-Invoicing**: Automatically prepares cycle invoices at 6:00 AM on scheduled monthly dates.
* **Direct Gmail Dispatch**: Send invoices as official PDF attachments straight from your connected Google account in 1 click.

---

### 2. Work Log & Video Deliverable Tracking
* **Native Video Previews**: Built-in modal player to preview YouTube, Vimeo, and Google Drive links directly inside the app without jumping tabs.
* **1-Click Google Drive Upload**: Upload video deliverables straight to Google Drive from your work log.
* **Sub-Client & Agency Support**: Tag individual projects and reels under sub-clients or parent agency accounts.
* **Bulk Actions**: Select multiple finished work items and instantly convert them into a single itemized invoice.
* **Live Status Tracking**: Instant visual clarity on what's **Uninvoiced** vs. **Invoiced** vs. **Paid**.

---

### 3. Client Management & Automated Overdue Reminders
* **30-Day Payment Cycle Monitoring**: Automatically tracks next due dates and flags overdue accounts.
* **1-Click WhatsApp Reminders**: Generate pre-formatted, polite payment reminder messages with total pending balance and UPI details ready to send on WhatsApp with one click.
* **1-Click Email Reminders**: Instant mailto drafts pre-filled with client deliverables, payment due date, and bank transfer information.
* **Custom Rate Cards**: Set default rates per reel, on-site shoot rates, and website design rates per client.
* **Client Archive / Reopen**: Close completed clients to keep your workspace tidy while preserving complete billing history.

---

### 4. Branded Client Onboarding & Intake Hub
* **Custom Intake Link & QR Code**: Share your branded URL (`/onboarding/:freelancerId`) with prospective clients.
* **Structured Creative Briefs**: Clients submit company details, contact person, project scope, proposed rates, and Instagram handles.
* **1-Click Convert**: Review incoming inquiries in your Onboarding pipeline and click **"Accept & Add Client"** to instantly generate their client profile and rate card.
* **Testimonials & Verified Reviews**: Collect 5-star testimonials with feedback and showcase them to prospective clients.

---

### 5. Client Portal (Read-Only Live Dashboard for Clients)
* **Real-time Transparency**: Clients get their own portal (`/portal/:clientId`) to view all work delivered, pending deliverables, and historical invoices.
* **Builds Client Trust**: No more "Hey, what did we work on this month?"—clients can see their complete project statement 24/7.

---

### 6. Freelancer Financial Dashboard & Milestones
* **Real-Time Revenue Analytics**: Track monthly earnings, pending receivables, and overall income growth at a glance.
* **Career Milestones & Streaks**: Celebrate freelancing anniversaries (e.g., "240 days worked") and project delivery streaks.
* **CSV Export & Backup**: Complete 1-click export of clients, work logs, and financial records for offline backup or tax filing.

---

### 7. Professional SaaS Aesthetic & Security
* **Restrained, Distraction-Free UI**: Flat, professional SaaS interface engineered for daily productivity.
* **Full Dark & Light Mode**: Seamless dark mode tailored for late-night editing sessions.
* **Private & Secure**: Powered by Google Firebase Firestore with strict per-user database rules. Your client data and financials remain 100% private.
