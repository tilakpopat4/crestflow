Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CrestFlow ("we," "our," "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application located at https://clients-management-tilak.vercel.app (the "Service").

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a better, more automated experience for your freelancing workflows.

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture URL, and unique Google ID.
  • Database Information: Any client directory records, project descriptions, work logs, billing history, and custom invoice data that you choose to store in CrestFlow. This information is stored securely in Firebase/Firestore.
  • Google API User Data (Gmail Scopes): As part of the client management features, you can authorize CrestFlow to interact with Gmail. This grants us permission to compose and send emails on your behalf.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure

CrestFlow's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, our integration only uses Gmail permissions (gmail.send and gmail.compose) to send and format invoice emails directly to your selected clients at your explicit action. We do not read your inbox, store your email logs, or transfer your email content to third parties or marketing platforms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only to operate and improve the Service:

  • To authenticate you and associate your data with your private user profile.
  • To display your clients, invoice statuses, and work logs.
  • To automate the process of composing and sending PDF invoices to your clients via Gmail when you choose to use the "Send via Gmail" feature.
  • To analyze and monitor system performance and improve usability.

4. Data Sharing and Transfer

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information, database information, or Google User Data to outside parties. Your data is stored directly in your designated Firestore database. No third-party servers receive your credentials or email data.

5. Data Security

We implement standard security measures to safeguard your information. User data is protected by Firebase Authentication and Firestore Security Rules to prevent unauthorized read or write access.

6. Data Deletion and Control

You have full control over your data. You can delete items directly within the dashboard. If you wish to delete your entire CrestFlow account and all associated Firestore records permanently, please contact us at tilakpopat2007@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
