Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Overview

This Cancellation and Refund Policy applies to subscriptions and software services provided by Tilak Popat Films ("CrestFlow", "we", "us", or "our"). By purchasing a subscription to CrestFlow Pro, you agree to the terms outlined below.

2. Subscription Plans & Billing

CrestFlow offers a Free tier with core limits (up to 2 clients and 3 invoices/month) and a premium Pro plan:

  • CrestFlow Pro Monthly: ₹79 per month (billed monthly).
  • CrestFlow Pro Yearly: ₹799 per year (billed annually).

Subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each billing cycle unless cancelled prior to the renewal date.

3. Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your subscription at any time without penalty or cancellation fees:

  • You may cancel directly inside your CrestFlow account under Settings > Profile > Subscription by clicking "Cancel Subscription".
  • When you cancel, your Pro access remains active until the end of your current paid billing period.
  • No further automatic recurring renewals or deductions will take place once cancellation is confirmed.
  • After your billing period concludes, your account reverts to the Free tier with your existing data preserved.

4. Refund Policy

  • 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee: If you are not satisfied with CrestFlow Pro, you can request a full refund within 7 calendar days of your initial subscription purchase or subsequent renewal.
  • Requests after 7 days: Refund requests made after 7 calendar days from the transaction date are not eligible for a refund, but you will retain full Pro access until your current period expires.
  • Duplicate Transactions: If you were accidentally charged twice for the same billing cycle due to a technical error, we will promptly refund the duplicate transaction in full upon notice.

5. Direct Invoice UPI Payments (Third-Party Client Payments)

CrestFlow features direct UPI QR code generation on invoices to allow freelancers to collect funds directly from their own clients into their personal or business bank accounts. CrestFlow does not act as a payment gateway, escrow service, or merchant of record for transactions between you and your clients. Disputes, chargebacks, or refund requests between freelancers and their clients must be handled directly between the freelancer and their client.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund for your CrestFlow Pro subscription, please email us with:

  • Your registered account email address.
  • Your Razorpay payment ID or invoice number.
  • A brief explanation of why the service did not meet your expectations.

Approved refunds are processed via Razorpay to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
